Engagements

Selected research and litigation-support work, described in general terms to protect client and subject confidentiality.

Confidential witnesses and interviews

Located and approached sensitive sources for litigation teams, including individuals who had left public-sector and corporate roles. Conducted preliminary interviews, verified identities, and documented chain-of-contact so attorneys could evaluate credibility before deciding whether to depose or call a witness.

Biotech and pharmaceutical disputes

Tracked patent filings, FDA correspondence, clinical-trial records, and regulatory complaints for counsel in life-sciences litigation. Identified expert candidates and mapped relationships between researchers, institutions, and funding sources.

Securities and shareholder litigation

Researched public filings, executive histories, related-party transactions, and market disclosures for class-action and derivative matters. Helped counsel connect corporate events to trading patterns and draft discovery targets.

Foreign companies and cross-border assets

Traced offshore entities, beneficial owners, and asset movements through corporate registries, customs records, litigation databases, and open-source research. Worked under attorney direction to preserve privilege and avoid conflict with local data laws.

General private-investigation support

Handled skip tracing, background checks, asset discovery, and digital-footprint analysis for attorneys, individuals, and corporate clients. Delivered sourced memoranda with clear confidence levels and limitations noted.
